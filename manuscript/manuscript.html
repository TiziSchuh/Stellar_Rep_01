Keywords

Replication, Awe, Humility

The seminal findings of the Open Science Collaboration (2015) triggered what is now known as the ‘replication crisis’ in psychological science, prompting researchers to question thusfar widely unchallenged publications by and large. Since then, there has been an onging effort within the psychological research community to actively persue replication of affected studies with important findings, aiming to reevaluate their validity and ultimately restore trust in the body of psychological knowledge.

The Aim of this Replicating Study

As part of the Collaborative Replications and Education Project (CREP) project of Grahe et al. (2026), we aim to contribute to endeavour descirbed above, providing a replication study of ‘Study 3’ in Stellar et al. (2018): Awe and Humility – details regarding theoretical background and study design are outlined in the original publication. To ensure transparency, following the general recommendation of adopting the principles of open science (Schönbrodt et al. 2018), we have made all relevant materials available on the platform Open Science Framework (OSF).

Method

The scripts required for reproducing the following results have been made publicaly available on GitHub at https://github.com/TiziSchuh/Stellar_Rep_01.

The hypotheses, operationalizations, analysis plan, inference criteria, and sample size justification have been preregistered at https://osf.io/537aq. Open material and data can all be found at https://osf.io/cy287.
