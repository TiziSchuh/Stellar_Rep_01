The seminal findings of the Open Science Collaboration
(\citeproc{ref-open2015estimating}{2015}) triggered what is now known as
the `replication crisis' in psychological science, prompting researchers
to question thusfar widely unchallenged publications by and large. Since
then, there has been an onging effort within the psychological research
community to actively persue replication of affected studies with
important findings, aiming to reevaluate their validity and ultimately
restore trust in the body of psychological knowledge.

\subsection{The Aim of this Replicating
Study}\label{the-aim-of-this-replicating-study}

As part of the Collaborative Replications and Education Project (CREP)
project of Grahe et al. (\citeproc{ref-grahe2026crep}{2026}), we aim to
contribute to endeavour descirbed above, providing a replication study
of `Study 3' in Stellar et al. (\citeproc{ref-stellar2018awe}{2018}):
Awe and Humility -- details regarding theoretical background and study
design are outlined in the original publication. To ensure transparency,
following the general recommendation of adopting the principles of open
science (\citeproc{ref-schonbrodt2018forschungstransparenz}{Schönbrodt
et al., 2018}), we have made all relevant materials available on the
platform Open Science Framework (OSF).

\section{Method}\label{method}

The hypotheses, operationalizations, analysis plan, inference criteria,
and sample size justification have been preregistered at
\url{https://osf.io/537aq}. Open material and data are available at
\url{https://osf.io/cy287}.

\subsection{Participants}\label{participants}

The initial sample had 189 participants. We preregistered to exclude
participants who did not write anything in the writing assignment (26
exclusions) or indicated that their data should not be used (1
exclusions), leading to a final sample size of \emph{n} = 162. The final
sample had a mean age of 32 (\emph{SD} = 16.2, range: 18--77).
